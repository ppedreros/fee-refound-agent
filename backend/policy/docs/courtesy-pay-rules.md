# Courtesy Pay and Overdraft Rules

## 1. What Courtesy Pay is

Courtesy Pay is when the credit union pays an item, such as a card payment or a bill, that the account balance can't cover. The account goes below zero, and a Courtesy Pay fee is charged for the item.

## 2. The nightly posting order

Each night, the core system posts the day's transactions one by one, in its own order. A deposit that arrives the same day can post after a payment, so the balance can dip below zero for a moment even though the money arrived that day.

## 3. One fee per item

A Courtesy Pay fee is charged once for each item paid into overdraft, never twice for the same item.
