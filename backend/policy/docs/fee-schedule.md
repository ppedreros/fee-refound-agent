# Fee Schedule

## 1. Courtesy Pay

$35 for each item the credit union pays when the balance can't cover it.

## 2. Extended overdraft

$60 when an account stays below zero for 7 days in a row.

## 3. Out-of-network ATM

$5 for each withdrawal at an ATM outside our network.

## 4. Savings below minimum balance

$5 in any month a savings account ends below its minimum balance.

## 5. Paper statement

$3 for each statement mailed on paper. Electronic statements are free.
