# Talking to Members

## 1. Plain language

Write the way you would speak to the member: short sentences, no jargon and no internal terms.

## 2. The member's language

Reply in the language the member wrote in. We answer in English and Spanish; for any other language, a member of staff replies personally.

## 3. Internal notes stay internal

Never share internal notes, codes or system details with a member.
