# Fee Refund Policy

## 1. Scope

Service fees can be refunded as a courtesy, under the rules below. A member of staff approves every refund; no refund is automatic.

## 2. How many refunds

Members in good standing can receive up to 3 fee refunds in any 12-month period. The 12 months end on the date of the fee being reviewed, and refunds of any fee type count.

## 3. Good standing

A member is in good standing when there is no unpaid balance on any account. The Account Standing policy explains what counts as unpaid.

## 4. Same-day deposits

We refund a Courtesy Pay fee when a deposit that posted the same day would have covered the payment if it had posted first. Refunds and transfers between the member's own accounts don't count as deposits.

## 5. One refund per fee

Each fee can be refunded only once. A fee that was already refunded, in this tool or in the core system, is not refunded again.
